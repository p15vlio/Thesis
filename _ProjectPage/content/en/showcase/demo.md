The videos below show the [main scenarios](/gettingStarted/flows) on the running system, with the demo data. The web panel was recorded in a browser and the kiosk application on an Android tablet emulator. Because the emulator has no card reader, a test service that produces the card events was installed on it for the recording; that service is not part of the system. The steps run automatically and the videos have no sound.

All the scenarios are also available as one continuous video: [the whole demo (about 11 minutes)](/media/videos/GuestDesk_demo_full.mp4).

## Platform administrator

The dashboard, the owners, the form for creating an owner, the view of a single owner's data, the records of the whole platform, the audit log and the settings.

![The platform administrator in the web panel](/media/videos/clips/01_admin.mp4)

## Owner

Signing in, editing the property together with the information for the guest, adding a device with its QR code and activation code, the orders, the products and the delivery platforms.

![The owner in the web panel](/media/videos/clips/02_owner.mp4)

## Kiosk device activation

The tablet is activated with the code the owner created in the previous video. The idle screen, the home screen and the stay information follow.

![Activating the kiosk device](/media/videos/clips/03_activation.mp4)

## Order from the in-room store

The guest completes an order from the property's own store. On the right, the owner sees the order and moves it forward. The tablet follows the status changes, and the cancel button is no longer shown once preparation has started.

![In-room store order: the kiosk on the left, the owner's web panel on the right](/media/videos/clips/04_store.mp4)

## Food order

An order from a restaurant on a food delivery platform. The simulated platform updates the order's status by itself until delivery, and the changes appear in both applications.

![Food order: the kiosk on the left, the owner's web panel on the right](/media/videos/clips/05_food.mp4)

## Cancellation by the guest

The guest cancels the order before preparation starts, and the owner sees it as cancelled.

![Cancellation by the guest: the kiosk on the left, the owner's web panel on the right](/media/videos/clips/06_cancel.mp4)
